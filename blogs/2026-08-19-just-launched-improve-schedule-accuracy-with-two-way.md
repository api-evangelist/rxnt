By automating appointment management, practices can spend less time managing busy schedules. Staff gain greater visibility into schedule changes, patients will have a more convenient experience, and the office can make better use of valuable provider availability. No-shows cost the healthcare industry more than $150 billion each year—and some practices get hit harder than others.
