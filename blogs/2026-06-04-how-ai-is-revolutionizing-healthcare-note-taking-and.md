The AI healthcare scribe represents the next evolution in medical documentation technology, helping providers reclaim time & improve accuracy. Healthcare providers spend nearly two hours on documentation for every hour of patient care. This administrative burden has reached a breaking point, contributing to widespread physician burnout and reduced quality of patient interactions.
