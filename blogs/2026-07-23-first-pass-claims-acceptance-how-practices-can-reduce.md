First-pass claims acceptance rate measures how often claims are accepted by payers on the first submission without corrections, rework, or resubmission. Improving this rate can help you reduce billing delays and burden on your administrative team. You can also strengthen your clinic’s cash flow by preventing avoidable errors before claims ever leave your system.
