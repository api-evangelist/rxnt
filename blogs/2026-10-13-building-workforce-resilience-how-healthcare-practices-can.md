Workforce resilience includes creating systems, workflows, and workplace practices that help teams manage their responsibilities sustainably. Key takeaways Individual resilience often refers to someone’s ability to adapt to challenges and recover from stressful experiences. Workforce resilience applies those same skills within a work environment.
